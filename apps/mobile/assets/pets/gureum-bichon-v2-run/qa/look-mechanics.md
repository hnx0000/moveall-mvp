# Gureum Bichon look mechanics

The lower torso and paws remain registered to one baseline. The large fluffy head leads each gaze, the glossy eyes turn with the head rather than sliding as detached pupils, the nose shifts toward the target side, and the soft ears and tail follow by a smaller amount.

- 000 up: chin lifts; pupils and nose rise; both ear bottoms expose evenly; torso stays front-facing.
- 090 screen-right: muzzle, nose, and pupils shift to screen-right; the screen-left cheek becomes more visible and the far ear compresses slightly.
- 180 down: chin tucks; upper eyelids lower; nose and pupils move down; head squashes subtly toward the chest.
- 270 screen-left: muzzle, nose, and pupils shift to screen-left; the screen-right cheek becomes more visible and the far ear compresses slightly.

Each 22.5-degree step uses roughly one quarter of the adjacent cardinal head turn. The body stays anchored; the head, eyes, muzzle, ears, and tail progress continuously. No whole-sprite rotation, no replacement eyes, no scale changes, and no detached effects.
